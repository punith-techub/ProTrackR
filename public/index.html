Sign in with Google Setup

Google Sign-In is completely implemented! To enable the Google account popup for your users:

  1. Open .env in your project root.
  2. Set GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=your_id.apps.googleusercontent.com (100% free from Google Cloud Console).
  3. Add http://localhost:3000 to your Authorized JavaScript Origins.
